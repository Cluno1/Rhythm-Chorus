# Sonorus brand assets

`assets/brand/sonorus-master.png` is the version-controlled copy of the user-supplied 1024×1024 Sonorus master received on 2026-09-05. It is the sole visual source for this repository.

The Android symbol, adaptive foreground, monochrome icon, notification icon, splash/About image, widgets, legacy mipmaps, README icon, and wordmark are mechanical crops, transparency/color conversions, safe-area padding, and resizes of that master. Their outline must not be redrawn or modified. Product text uses `Sonorus`; the wordmark inside the master remains lowercase `sonorus`.

The project maintainers are responsible for the rights to the Sonorus brand artwork. Upstream Rhythm artwork and naming are not used as the Sonorus product identity.
